## Search record and stopping reason

Twelve research waves and final spot checks covered event rules, direct recruiters, mock interviews, assessments, technical platforms, roleplay training, public hackathon projects, repositories, adjacent simulations, hiring research, dialogue maps, developer documentation, pricing, candidate discussion and data handling. Raw tool results and their native reference metadata are preserved in retrieval-log.json. Queries are recorded in the conversation tool history. No product account was created and no hands-on product demo was completed.

Stop broad research because the strongest candidate directions have identified competitors, original evidence for the relevant work, plausible technical paths, and explicit limits. Further broad search is unlikely to remove the need for buyer and candidate validation. Current event submission inventory and exact current deployment permissions remain unresolved.
